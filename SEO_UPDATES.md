# SEO updates — broader tyre searches

This revision keeps the website focused on the products actually offered: BKT Off-The-Road (OTR) tyres for construction, mining, earthmoving, road construction and industrial equipment. It does not claim to sell passenger-car or motorcycle tyres.

## Changes
- Broadened homepage title, description and visible copy to include tyre searches alongside BKT OTR terms.
- Added a dedicated `/tyres` landing page for tyre-related searches, with links to the published application pages, catalogue and quote form.
- Added `/tyres` to `app/sitemap.ts`.
- Added `/tyres` links to desktop and mobile navigation and the homepage footer.
- Updated metadata for the catalogue, quote page and locations page to include relevant generic tyre terms.

## After deploying
1. Open `https://bkt-web.vercel.app/tyres` and confirm it loads.
2. Open `https://bkt-web.vercel.app/sitemap.xml` and confirm `/tyres` appears in the XML.
3. In Search Console, inspect `https://bkt-web.vercel.app/tyres` and request indexing if the live test passes.
4. Keep the sitemap submitted; do not repeatedly resubmit it unless the report shows an issue.

SEO changes cannot guarantee rankings, and the broad search term `tyres` is highly competitive. The strongest realistic targets are specific, relevant phrases such as `OTR tyres Uttar Pradesh`, `heavy-duty tyres Gorakhpur`, `construction equipment tyres`, `mining tyres`, and `BKT tyres`.
